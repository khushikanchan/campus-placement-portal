# PROJECT LIFECYCLE – SIX PHASES

**Selected seminar framework:** the six-phase sequence requested for this presentation. Benefits realization may be part of closure or post-project governance in other methods.

## Slide outline and speaker notes

| Slide | Content | Suggested speaking notes | Time |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1. Title | Project Lifecycle – Six Phases · Campus Placement Portal case | Introduce the topic and explain that each phase will be connected to one campus system. | 0:30 |
| 2. Why a lifecycle? | An idea becomes a tested service through deliberate phases. | A lifecycle gives stakeholders checkpoints, ownership and a way to manage change. | 0:45 |
| 3. Six phases at a glance | Initiation → Planning → Execution ↔ Monitoring & Controlling → Closure → Benefits Review | Monitoring runs continuously alongside execution. Benefits are measured after delivery. | 0:50 |
| 4. Initiation | Problem, need, goals, stakeholders, feasibility, charter, initial scope | Our problem is fragmented placement communication. Students, recruiters and TPO are key stakeholders. | 0:55 |
| 5. Planning | Requirements, WBS, schedule, resources, risks, communication, sprints | Translate the three roles' needs into epics, stories and a realistic two-sprint plan. | 0:55 |
| 6. Execution | Build, coordinate, test, communicate, deliver | Implement authentication and profiles before connecting job applications, interviews and reports. | 0:55 |
| 7. Monitoring & Controlling | Measure progress, scope, time, quality, risks; correct deviations | Monitoring compares actual work with the plan, then drives action while work continues. | 0:55 |
| 8. Monitoring loop | Plan → Execute → Monitor → Compare → Identify deviation → Correct → Update plan → Continue | Show the loop as recurring; a sprint review provides evidence and replanning. | 0:50 |
| 9. Closure | Final tests, acceptance, docs, handover, lessons, closure report | Confirm the main student, recruiter and TPO workflows with faculty before handover. | 0:50 |
| 10. Benefits review | Measure use, participation, time saved, satisfaction, improvements | After launch, compare baseline placement effort and student participation against results. | 0:50 |
| 11. One project, six phases | Portal example mapped phase by phase | The same project objective guides charter, build choices, acceptance and post-launch measures. | 1:00 |
| 12. Challenges & lessons | Data quality, security, scope, integration, adoption | Stage delivery, protect student records, and gather feedback early to avoid late rework. | 0:55 |
| 13. Conclusion | Clear lifecycle improves delivery and accountability | Recap the six phases and emphasize continuous monitoring during execution. | 0:35 |
| 14. Q&A | Questions | Invite questions; team members can take questions on their assigned phases. | 0:20 |

**Estimated presentation time:** about 11 minutes, plus questions.

## Monitoring loop diagram

```text
PLAN → EXECUTE → MONITOR → COMPARE ACTUAL WITH PLAN
  ↑                                         ↓
UPDATE PLAN ← TAKE CORRECTIVE ACTION ← IDENTIFY DEVIATION
  └──────────────── continue execution ────────────────┘
```
